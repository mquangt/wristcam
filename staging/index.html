WristCam

Camera wrist trainer. Set your phone up, swing, and hear when your lead wrist is out of position at P2, P3, the top and beyond.

Camera setup
  • Down the line: phone at hand height, 2.5–3.5 m behind you, on your target line, landscape or portrait with your whole body and club in frame.
  • Face on: phone level with your hands, 2.5–3 m in front of you. Wrist cupping/bowing reads best from here.
  • Keep the phone level and still (a tripod or bag stand helps). Good light, no backlight.
  • Address the ball and hold still for half a second: the app arms itself, then swing.
  • Drill mode beeps continuously while you rehearse slowly or hold a position. Swing mode beeps at each checkpoint during a full swing.
  • Turn the volume up. On iPhone it plays even with the silent switch on (iOS 16.4+).

Camera readings are estimates from a single 2D video. Use the presets as a starting point and the "Set target from this position" button to calibrate to your own swing.
